# Privacy Policy for Amar Bangla (আমার বাংলা)

**Last Updated:** October 2026  
**Extension Name:** Amar Bangla - আমার বাংলা (অফলাইন বানান ও ব্যাকরণ)  
**Developer Contact:** [Contact Developer](https://github.com/JowelislamHabib) / [Support Kori](https://www.supportkori.com/jowelislam)

---

## 1. Overview
**Amar Bangla (আমার বাংলা)** is an offline, privacy-first Bengali spell checker, grammar checker, and dictionary tool for Google Chrome. We believe your privacy is fundamental. This extension is designed from the ground up to operate completely on your local device without sending your text, keystrokes, or personal data to any external server.

---

## 2. Data Collection and Usage

### No Keystrokes or Text Data Collection
- **100% Offline Processing:** All text entered into input fields, textareas, and contenteditable areas is processed strictly in-memory on your local browser.
- **No Remote Transmission:** We do **NOT** transmit, upload, collect, log, or monitor any text or documents you write.
- **Zero Third-Party Analytics:** We do not employ Google Analytics, telemetry trackers, or external tracking scripts.

### Local Settings & Personal Dictionary
- **Chrome Storage API (`chrome.storage.sync`):** The extension uses Chrome's built-in storage solely to save:
  1. Your personal dictionary words (words you choose to add with the "+" button or ignore with "✕").
  2. Your user preferences (such as Dark/Light theme mode, grammar check toggle, and global extension toggle).
- This data remains within your private Google Chrome profile and is synchronized exclusively by Google according to your Google account settings. We never have access to this data.

---

## 3. Permissions Explanation
Amar Bangla requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:

- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to store your personal dictionary words and theme preferences locally across browser sessions.
- **`host_permissions` (`http://*/*`, `https://*/*`)**: Needed solely to inject the content script to detect misspelled Bengali words on web forms and text inputs you interact with. No web traffic or browsing history is accessed, read, or transmitted.
